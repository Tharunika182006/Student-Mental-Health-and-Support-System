About This Project

Addressing the Student Mental Health Crisis

Student mental health has reached a critical point, with depression, anxiety, and stress-related disorders affecting nearly 40% of university students worldwide. Many lack access to timely mental health support, while others struggle with digital distraction impacting their academic performance and wellbeing.

MindFlow is an innovative AI-powered solution designed specifically for students. By combining intelligent mood tracking, personalized AI counselling, stress assessment, focus optimization, and digital wellbeing tools, MindFlow creates a comprehensive support ecosystem that students can access anytime, anywhere.

This final-year project demonstrates how artificial intelligence and thoughtful design can address real-world mental health challenges. Our platform empowers students to take control of their emotional wellbeing while improving academic performance through better focus and stress management.

  • Evidence-based approach: All features developed with psychological research and best practices in mind.
  • Privacy-first design: Student data security and privacy are paramount in every implementation.
  • User-centric: Developed with continuous student feedback and real-world testing.
  • Scalable technology: Built with modern AI and cloud technology for reliability and growth.
  • Holistic wellbeing: Addresses mental health, digital wellness, and academic productivity simultaneously.
  • Continuous improvement: Machine learning models adapt to each student's unique needs over time.
